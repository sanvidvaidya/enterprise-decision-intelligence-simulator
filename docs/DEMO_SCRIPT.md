# 90-Second Demo Script

## 0 to 12 seconds: establish the product

Start on **Home** in Public Demo mode. Use the opening statement to frame the complete job: onboard governed data, judge exposure, understand evidence, negotiate scenarios, allocate capacity, and govern decisions. Point to the mode banner, isolated synthetic organization, live portfolio pulse, and highest-priority briefing.

## 12 to 25 seconds: portfolio decision

Open **Command Center**. Point to high-risk customers, high-risk ACV, renewals inside 90 days, and overdue actions. Explain that the queue combines operational risk with commercial exposure while keeping the two concepts visible.

## 25 to 40 seconds: evidence, not a black box

Open **Customer 360** and select a high-risk account such as Evergreen Retail. Show the score contribution chart. Expand a factor and point from its explanation to the exact usage, ticket, or contract source row.

## 40 to 50 seconds: explain movement

Open **Change Timeline**. Show the score trajectory, factor additions/removals/reweighting, evidence IDs, and the unified operational plus human-decision history.

## 50 to 60 seconds: test an intervention

Open **Scenario Lab** for the same account. Resolve critical tickets, recover inactive seats, or extend the renewal. Show the score change and emphasize that the scenario reuses the documented rules without changing source data.

## 60 to 70 seconds: allocate constrained resources

Open **Capacity Planner**. Change available hours or escalation slots and show funded versus deferred interventions, including the precise constraint reason.

## 70 to 80 seconds: govern the rules

Open **Policy Studio**. Change a weight and show the portfolio replay and reclassified ACV. Point to versioning and independent approval, then show that public visitors cannot persist a draft.

## 80 to 86 seconds: operationalize the answer

Open **Actions & Decisions**. Show the recommended intervention, owner, priority, and due date. Explain that Private Business mode records what humans actually choose, while Public Demo prevents shared visitors from changing the workflow.

## 86 to 90 seconds: prove data governance and portability

Open **Data Onboarding** to show the downloadable templates and technically disabled public uploader, then **Data Health** for synthetic ingestion history, row-level issues, and SHA-256 manifests. Close by explaining that the same repository provides a safe public demonstration and an explicit local Private Business mode without an LLM, paid API, model download, or Docker.
